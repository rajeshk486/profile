02 — How I work

Six principles I lead by.

01

Architecture is a leadership act.

The system you draw decides what your team can ship for the next two years. I treat design reviews as the highest-leverage hour in the week.

02

Ship under load, not in isolation.

Migrations get validated against the legacy system in parallel — continuously. Zero data loss is a process, not a hope.

03

Encode the toil.

If a task repeats weekly, it's a candidate for an agent. EM bandwidth belongs to high-judgement decisions, not to status compilation.

04

Compliance is a feature.

FinTech taught me to design for audit, mandate flow, and recovery from day one — not to retrofit them when regulators ask.

05

Frameworks earn their keep.

I'll reach for Spring Boot or Vertex AI without flinching — and just as readily build a multi-agent orchestrator from scratch when no framework fits.

06

Lead AI-natively.

The leaders who'll matter in this decade aren't the ones managing AI teams. They're the ones whose own workflow is built with AI from the inside out.
